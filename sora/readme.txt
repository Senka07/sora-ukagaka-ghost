Sora - a shy girl on your desktop
=================================

A small English-speaking Ukagaka ghost.

Install
-------
1. Get SSP (http://ssp.shillest.net/) and start it.
2. Drag sora.nar onto the character on your desktop and confirm.
   On Linux, SSP runs under Wine (X11 + a compositor for transparency).

What she does
-------------
- Greets you depending on the time of day, says goodbye when closed.
- Talks on her own every few minutes (adjustable in her menu).
- Tells the date, the time and how long the PC has been on.
- Announces the hour.
- Double-click her: menu. Double-click head / chest / face: reactions.
- Move the mouse over her head for a while to pat her.
